## Outcome

The five profiles contain useful source-based observations, but several original summaries overstate what those observations establish. The main weaknesses are promoting announced intentions into actions, substituting policy or organisational performance for the specific leadership dimension, and failing to display a material qualifying finding prominently enough.

All thirty dimensions were reviewed. Decisions concern the qualitative paragraphs and their evidence, not approval of leadership scores.

## What was checked

Every draft paragraph was compared with its structured evidence claims, cited source limitations, period scope and the original six dimension definitions. Selected original passages were reread where wording or a qualification was decisive. The exact scope and source IDs are recorded in `review-decisions.json`.

This is not a claim that all external documents were read exhaustively again. Existing source-capture and integrity results were retained; no new external retrieval was made. The content checks were performed by the original drafting assistant and cannot become an independent review merely because they are repeated or commissioned by the user.

## Material corrections

### Statements and actions

Starmer's disorder speech records stated support for targeted communities and enforcement. The original summary's phrase about protecting communities could be read as an established action or outcome. The revised text states precisely what the speech establishes and what remains to be demonstrated. Similarly, a departure speech establishes statements about succession; it does not verify that every transfer action was lawful or competently completed.

### Fit to Crisis

The original Crisis dimension concerns composure under pressure and calm communication. Aid pledges, subsequent review commissioning, apologies and programme success or failure can provide context, but do not themselves establish those qualities. The Johnson, Burnham, Obama and Scholz Crisis summaries are therefore narrowed and marked insufficient for a personal dimension assessment. Starmer's summary is amended, with the immigration speech retained as conditional communication context rather than automatically classified as an acute crisis case.

This does not find that any leader lacked composure. It finds that the current packet does not adequately support that judgement in either direction. No new dimension or disqualification rule is introduced.

### Geidt's maintained finding and disclosure criticism

The original Johnson Service summary did not make Geidt's specific maintained no-conflict assessment sufficiently visible. Rereading the original 17 December 2021 letter confirms that Geidt maintained the fundamental assessment that the interests created by the payment did not produce a conflict or reasonably perceived conflict, while criticising disclosure and qualifying other conclusions.

New claim **IR-BJ01**, extracted from the already retained **NBJ02** original, records that qualification. It is added to Process and Service as qualifying evidence. It is neither general exoneration nor a reason to omit the adverse disclosure findings. The revised summaries show both.

### Service and personal benefit

Rules applying to appointees, a salary pledge, a declared interest and statements of constitutional duty have different evidential strength. None automatically establishes sustained personal sacrifice or absence of self-interest. The Burnham, Obama and Scholz Service assessments remain insufficient, and their revised text identifies the missing personal-conduct record.

The meeting declaration is not recast as proof of a personal financial conflict or recusal. The reported payment or pledge is not upgraded to an audited recurring contribution. An empty adverse-evidence category is not treated as evidence that no adverse conduct exists.

### Personal attribution and balance

The Foreign Office participation study is institutional context, not direct evidence of Scholz's empathy. Coalition breakdown alone does not establish policy incoherence. External analysis noting the absence of an apology in one speech does not prove a personality trait. These distinctions are made explicit in the corrected Scholz text.

The PCLOB disagreements remain visible, and the majority's conclusion remains attributed to that institution. Reviewing dissent does not erase the majority findings or establish that a disputed programme was lawful. Positive family evidence in Kerslake remains alongside reported support gaps and the limits of personal attribution.

## Why scores remain open

The review does not infer numerical proposals from acceptance of a paragraph. Source sufficiency for a bounded observation differs from sufficiency for a whole-period ordinal judgement. All profiles retain material gaps in coverage, attribution, follow-through or interpretation. Zero remains an assessed judgement and is never used for those gaps. No incomplete total is calculated.

This is not withholding optional proposals solely because the rubric is still proposed. It is a substantive evidence judgement about the current packet. No additional approval from the user is needed to continue accessible research and corrections already within scope. Publishing an official edition remains a separate authorised editorial action.

## Disposition

The internal review is complete and its corrections are applied. The profiles remain research drafts. Human sign-off, independent review, operational-rubric approval and publisher approval are unfilled. No CMS data, public website content, production branch or deployment was changed.
