## People

**Internal decision: Amend.** Acknowledging responsibility for an operation is not a direct measure of empathy, and surveillance findings concern public impacts before individual listening is established. Avoid combining unlike evidence into a positive generalisation.

**Revised draft:** The packet records communication to bereaved communities and acceptance of responsibility for hostage deaths and health-rollout failures. Those are specific citizen-facing acts, with implementation and surveillance concerns requiring separate consideration. They do not yet establish sustained listening or responsiveness across both terms.
