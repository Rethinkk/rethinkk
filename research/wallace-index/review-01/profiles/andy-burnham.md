# Andy Burnham — draft leadership profile

**For human research review — not an official RETHINKK assessment.** Prepared 17 October 2026.

Office: Mayor of Greater Manchester, United Kingdom. Assessment scope: 2017-05-08 to 2026-06-19. Methodology: six original dimensions, version 1.0 proposed and unapproved. See the [coverage audit](../coverage-audit.md) for temporal and domain gaps.

No proposed numerical score is assigned where a material evidence gap remains. All totals are unassessed. The qualitative conclusions below are newly written research interpretations, not recovered prototype reasoning or independent reviewer findings.

## People

**Draft judgement:** Family-centred commissioning, positive accounts of personal visits and responses to victim concerns provide direct evidence of attention to people. Geographical gaps reported by families and serious victim-service failures prevent a one-sided conclusion.

### Supporting evidence

- **AB02-C1** — The independent review records that Burnham commissioned it and prioritised the experiences of bereaved families and other affected people. [AB02](https://www.greatermanchester-ca.gov.uk/media/1271/the-kerslake-report.pdf); Sections 1.2–1.3, printed pages 11–12.
- **AB02-C2** — The report records some respondents’ positive accounts of personal visits by the mayor and chief constable. [AB02](https://www.greatermanchester-ca.gov.uk/media/1271/the-kerslake-report.pdf); Section 2.22, printed pages 49–50.
- **NAB05-C1** — The statement accepted serious inspection issues and announced measures including a dedicated route for victims whose crimes were not recorded or appropriately handled. [NAB05](https://www.greatermanchester-ca.gov.uk/news/mayor-and-deputy-mayor-announce-actions-relating-to-hmicfrs-report-into-greater-manchester-police/); Report response and victim hotline paragraphs.
- **NAB06-C1** — The report quotes Burnham’s letter apologising for historical discrimination, his statement about operational independence and Tatchell’s view that a mayoral apology did not replace a force apology. [NAB06](https://www.the-independent.com/news/uk/home-news/andy-burnham-greater-manchester-police-lgbtq-apology-b2889684.html); Quoted letter and recipient response.

### Adverse or contradictory evidence

- **AB02-C3** — The report also records that some respondents outside Manchester felt overlooked or less well supported. This is not a finding of Burnham’s sole responsibility. [AB02](https://www.greatermanchester-ca.gov.uk/media/1271/the-kerslake-report.pdf); Section 2.23, printed page 50.
- **AB03-C1** — The inspection identified serious weaknesses in victim service, crime recording, investigation and governance, while recognising some improvements and a programme of change. [AB03](https://assets-hmicfrs.justiceinspectorates.gov.uk/uploads/an-inspection-of-the-service-provided-to-victims-of-crime-by-greater-manchester-police.pdf); Printed pages 8–12: summary, supervision and governance, causes of concern; inspection conducted September–October 2020.

### Qualifying evidence

- **NAB07-C1** — The interview reports Burnham’s salary pledge and questions him about homelessness, mental health and how support would work. [NAB07](https://www.bigissuenorth.com/features/2017/05/andy-burnham-interview/); Introduction and interview questions on the homelessness fund.

**Uncertainty and attribution:** Kerslake testimony is valuable but not a representative survey. Police-service failures must be connected to the mayor’s knowledge, powers and oversight rather than assigned personally by default.

**Evidence sufficiency:** enough for the bounded qualitative discussion above; insufficient for a complete-period numerical assessment. **Proposed score: Not assessed.** Material coverage, attribution or interpretation gaps described above remain unresolved. A number would imply a whole-period judgement not yet supported.
