# Barack Obama — draft leadership profile

**For human research review — not an official RETHINKK assessment.** Prepared 17 October 2026.

Office: President, United States. Assessment scope: 2009-01-20 to 2017-01-20. Methodology: six original dimensions, version 1.0 proposed and unapproved. See the [coverage audit](../coverage-audit.md) for temporal and domain gaps.

No proposed numerical score is assigned where a material evidence gap remains. All totals are unassessed. The qualitative conclusions below are newly written research interpretations, not recovered prototype reasoning or independent reviewer findings.

## People

**Draft judgement:** Communication after Newtown, responsibility for hostage deaths and acknowledgement of health-rollout failures provide direct citizen-facing evidence. Privacy concerns are material counterevidence to a uniformly favourable picture of attention to affected people.

### Supporting evidence

- **BO05-C1** — At the Newtown vigil Obama acknowledged bereaved families, praised responders and community support, and committed to work on preventing further shootings. [BO05](https://obamawhitehouse.archives.gov/the-press-office/2012/12/16/remarks-president-sandy-hook-interfaith-prayer-vigil); Speech at Newtown High School, passages on families and prevention.
- **NBO01-C1** — Obama accepted responsibility for counterterrorism operations, apologised to the families and described review of the operation that killed the hostages. [NBO01](https://obamawhitehouse.archives.gov/the-press-office/2015/04/23/statement-president-deaths-warren-weinstein-and-giovanni-lo-porto); Statement paragraphs on responsibility, apology and review.
- **NBO02-C1** — Obama accepted responsibility for the health-law rollout, acknowledged loss of credibility and discussed corrective action. [NBO02](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/DCPD-201300781/pdf/DCPD-201300781.pdf); DCPD-201300781, printed page 4 and subsequent health-marketplace answers.

### Adverse or contradictory evidence

- **BO03-C1** — The Board recommended ending bulk telephone-record collection, citing legal, privacy and effectiveness concerns. The report includes separate statements by two members. [BO03](https://www.judiciary.senate.gov/imo/media/doc/PCLOBReport.pdf); Printed page 16, Recommendation 1; introduction and annex contents.

### Qualifying evidence

- **BO07-C1** — The analysis reports changes in health insurance coverage and other indicators, and warns that numerical changes do not determine presidential credit or blame. [BO07](https://www.factcheck.org/2016/10/obamas-numbers-october-2016-update/); Analysis introduction and Health Insurance section.

**Uncertainty and attribution:** Speeches are not a representative record of listening, and outcomes are not personal empathy measures. Family experience, civilian-harm records and original coverage data need deeper review.

**Evidence sufficiency:** enough for the bounded qualitative discussion above; insufficient for a complete-period numerical assessment. **Proposed score: Not assessed.** Material coverage, attribution or interpretation gaps described above remain unresolved. A number would imply a whole-period judgement not yet supported.

## Process

**Draft judgement:** Ethics rules and transition instructions support institutional commitments; independent audit and majority surveillance findings supply substantial qualifications. The dissenting board members dispute important parts of the majority analysis.

### Supporting evidence

- **NBO04-C1** — The order requires an ethics pledge for appointees and contains restrictions, administration provisions and a written-waiver mechanism. [NBO04](https://obamawhitehouse.archives.gov/the-press-office/ethics-commitments-executive-branch-personnel/); Pledge, section 3 Waiver and administration provisions.
- **NBO03-C1** — Obama congratulated the president-elect and instructed his team to work towards a successful peaceful transition. [NBO03](https://obamawhitehouse.archives.gov/blog/2016/11/09/president-obama-speaks-results-election); Reproduced speech paragraphs on call, team instructions and transition.

### Adverse or contradictory evidence

- **BO02-C1** — GAO identified inadequate CMS planning and oversight, increased contract costs and delayed functionality. The record includes agency responses and later implementation of recommendations. [BO02](https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-14-694); What GAO Found; Recommendations for Executive Action.
- **BO03-C1** — The Board recommended ending bulk telephone-record collection, citing legal, privacy and effectiveness concerns. The report includes separate statements by two members. [BO03](https://www.judiciary.senate.gov/imo/media/doc/PCLOBReport.pdf); Printed page 16, Recommendation 1; introduction and annex contents.

### Qualifying evidence

- **BO03-C2** — Rachel Brand’s separate statement disagrees with ending the telephone-record programme without an adequate alternative and supports many other recommendations. [BO03](https://www.judiciary.senate.gov/imo/media/doc/PCLOBReport.pdf); Annex A, printed pages 208–213.
- **BO03-C3** — Elisebeth Collins Cook’s separate statement supports modifications but disputes the report’s statutory-authorisation, constitutional and efficacy analyses. [BO03](https://www.judiciary.senate.gov/imo/media/doc/PCLOBReport.pdf); Annex B, printed pages 214–218.
- **BO04-C1** — Obama set out announced intelligence reforms and further privacy work, presenting the need to balance security and civil liberties. [BO04](https://obamawhitehouse.archives.gov/the-press-office/2014/01/17/remarks-president-review-signals-intelligence/); Speech, reform proposals and closing discussion of privacy review.

**Uncertainty and attribution:** The Board was not unanimous on shutting down the programme or legal analysis. Ethical rules include waivers; neither their existence nor their mere availability establishes consistent conduct or abuse.

**Evidence sufficiency:** enough for the bounded qualitative discussion above; insufficient for a complete-period numerical assessment. **Proposed score: Not assessed.** Material coverage, attribution or interpretation gaps described above remain unresolved. A number would imply a whole-period judgement not yet supported.
