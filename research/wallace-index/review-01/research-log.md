## Preserved disagreements and contrary material

The PCLOB annex reading adds Rachel Brand's objection to ending the programme without an adequate alternative and Elisebeth Collins Cook's disagreements on authorisation, constitutional analysis and efficacy. This does not erase the majority's conclusions or establish the programme's legitimacy; it prevents a misleading presentation of unanimity.

Johnson's adviser letters and his reply are kept together, including the earlier qualified interests finding and the criticism of disclosure. Kerslake's positive family accounts remain alongside support gaps. Starmer's original immigration words remain alongside his later acknowledgement. Scholz's acceptance of the ruling remains alongside the invalidity finding, original coalition accusations and clearly labelled external analysis. A reader can therefore inspect what the draft interpretation weighs, rather than seeing only selected favourable or adverse items.
