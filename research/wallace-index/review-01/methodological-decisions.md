# Decisions requiring editorial resolution

These are explicit questions for human research review, not implemented changes or automatic publication gates beyond the agreed workflow. The six original dimensions, 0–3 integer scale and 18-point aggregate remain intact.

| Decision | Why it matters | Proposed editorial treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Approve or amend the proposed operational definitions | Version 1.0 is still proposed; published scores need an approved version | Review the existing rubric against these cases, record approval/changelog and retain historical versions |
| Weight severity and recurrence within a dimension | A severe institutional violation and many ordinary compliant acts cannot be combined by an unstated arithmetic rule | Explain a case-specific qualitative judgement and why adjacent descriptors are less appropriate; do not invent automatic disqualification or source-count thresholds |
| Define sufficient coverage for unequal terms | The scopes range from roughly two years to nine years and concern different offices | Require explicit sampling, institutional context and material-gap review, rather than identical incident quotas |
| Separate public acknowledgement from adequate correction | Apologies and regret are observable; sincerity and complete remedy are not automatically established | Examine scope, timing, subsequent decisions and contrary examples; avoid psychological diagnosis |
| Apply Service to benefits and duty | A pledge, disclosure, reported repayment, written rule and audited payment have different evidential strength | Keep those categories separate. Do not infer public-good motivation from policy agreement or personal ambition from merely holding office |
| Deal with duties and delegation | A mayor, PM, president and coalition chancellor have different authority | Record powers, advice, delegation, knowledge and actual decisions before attributing organisational findings |
| Handle disagreement within original evidence | The PCLOB majority and separate statements conflict materially | Preserve the arguments and remit. Do not count members or syndicated articles as evidential votes |
| Use later findings about earlier conduct | Publication can follow the assessment period | Record both dates and include only the identified in-period act; exclude later behaviour from the scored period |
| Resolve historical document identity | The supposed November 2024 code URL supplies an October 2025 title page | Find a reliable contemporaneous copy or archive before treating its contents as November 2024 rules; retain the conflicting retrieval |
| Define the contribution of missing evidence | Unknown is different from an adverse judgement | Keep the dimension null while a material gap remains; never turn missing information into zero or calculate an incomplete total |

No numerical proposal has been added in this packet. This follows the documented material gaps, not a requirement for prior approval merely to prepare a proposal. When those gaps are resolved, proposals may be prepared under the existing draft rubric, clearly labelled and subject to human review. Official publication still requires rubric approval, independent review and publisher sign-off.

No democratic-safeguard additions, exclusion rules or ideological criteria are proposed or implemented here. If such governance questions arise later, they require a separate document and must not silently alter the original model.
