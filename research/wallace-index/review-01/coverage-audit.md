## Priorities by evidential need

For **Humility**, the new original material establishes several specific acknowledgements: Johnson's apologies, Obama's responsibility statements, and Starmer's published interview. Burnham's 2020 statement is direct; his 2025 letter is currently available through reporting rather than the retrieved original. Scholz's election-result acknowledgement is reported and his court response is direct. These are evidence about conduct in particular episodes. They do not establish personality traits or consistent behaviour across all settings.

For **Service**, the packet now includes a salary pledge and a reported official contribution account, ethics-order provisions including waivers, specific interests declarations, reported repayment, adviser correspondence and succession conduct. Those are more relevant than an assumption that desirable policy outcomes prove selflessness. Audited personal-benefit management and actual long-term compliance remain major gaps. No adverse finding has been invented to fill an empty category.

For **People**, affected-family evidence in Kerslake is kept alongside accounts of support gaps; organisational participation studies are not attributed personally to a head of government. For **Process**, original adverse findings are kept separate from allegations, replies, questions and legal interpretation. For **Policy**, an articulated strategy is distinct from implementation and ideological agreement. For **Crisis**, visible communication is distinct from composure, sound decisions and effects; uncertainty and information available at the time still matter.

## Scope exclusions

- Burnham's ministerial career and any subsequent national office are outside the selected mayoral period. Historical police abuses are background; his in-period acknowledgement can be examined without attributing the earlier abuses to him.
- Earlier Hamburg and finance-minister events cannot become scored Scholz chancellorship conduct without an identified in-period act.
- Johnson's later conduct before the Committee is excluded from the premiership score, while later findings about in-period statements remain admissible retrospective evidence.
- Electoral success, defeat, vote shares and party affiliation are not leadership indicators. The Scholz record is relevant only to acknowledgement and constitutional conduct.
- Self-reported achievement lists in opening or departure speeches are not accepted as verified outcomes.

## Sufficiency judgement

The material supports thirty bounded qualitative drafts, but not complete-period numerical judgements. Each profile states its own reason. The remaining work is substantive research and human adjudication, not obtaining a fixed number of sources. An editor can review the drafts now, amend the evidence classifications and decide what additional work is material before requesting numerical proposals.
