### BJ02 — [R (Miller) v The Prime Minister; Cherry v Advocate General for Scotland](https://caselaw.nationalarchives.gov.uk/uksc/2019/41)

UK Supreme Court; The National Archives. Publication: 2019-09-24. Access attempt: 2026-10-17. Review: `relevant_passages_reviewed`; access: `retrieved`.

The Court held the advice to prorogue Parliament unlawful and the resulting prorogation null and without effect.

Locator: [2019] UKSC 41, paragraphs 50, 61 and 69–70.

Limitations: A legal finding about the exercise of a power. Do not turn it into a finding of criminality or a general character judgement. Examine the recorded government arguments and subsequent compliance.

### BJ03 — [Matter referred on 21 April 2022 (conduct of Rt Hon Boris Johnson): Final Report — Summary](https://publications.parliament.uk/pa/cm5803/cmselect/cmprivi/564/summary.html)

House of Commons Committee of Privileges; UK Parliament. Publication: 2023-06-15. Access attempt: 2026-10-17. Review: `official_summary_reviewed`; access: `retrieved`.

The Committee concluded that Johnson deliberately misled the House. The summary records its reasoning, his denials and his response to provisional conclusions.

Locator: Summary, paragraphs 11–17.

Limitations: Later finding partly concerns statements during the premiership, but also later conduct. Exclude post-premiership conduct from the scored period. Read the full evidence, Johnson’s representations and the Commons decision before assessment.

### BJ04 — [Coronavirus: lessons learned to date report published](https://committees.parliament.uk/committee/135/science-and-technology-committee-commons/news/157991/coronavirus-lessons-learned-to-date-report-published/)

Commons Science and Technology Committee and Health and Social Care Committee; UK Parliament. Publication: 2021-10-12. Access attempt: 2026-10-17. Review: `official_summary_reviewed`; access: `retrieved`.

The committees identified both vaccination successes and serious weaknesses in the early pandemic response, including delayed lockdown and shortcomings affecting vulnerable groups.

Locator: Opening summary and principal findings.

Limitations: An official summary of a collective institutional response, not a personal verdict on Johnson. The underlying report, decision records and subsequent inquiry findings still require review.

### BJ05 — [Prime Minister pledges UK’s unwavering support to Ukraine on visit to Kyiv: 9 April 2022](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/prime-minister-pledges-uks-unwavering-support-to-ukraine-on-visit-to-kyiv-9-april-2022)

Prime Minister’s Office, 10 Downing Street; GOV.UK. Publication: 2022-04-09. Access attempt: 2026-10-17. Review: `relevant_passages_reviewed`; access: `retrieved`.

The government recorded Johnson’s Kyiv visit and announced additional military assistance and World Bank lending guarantees.

Locator: Paragraphs describing assistance and lending guarantees.

Limitations: Primary evidence of the announcement and stated policy. Verify delivery and the leader’s decision-making independently; the release does not establish motives or effectiveness.
