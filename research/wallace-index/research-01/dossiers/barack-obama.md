## Source records and limits

### BO01 — [President Barack Obama](https://obamalibrary.archives.gov/obamas/president-barack-obama)

Barack Obama Presidential Library, National Archives and Records Administration; NARA. Publication: not verified. Access attempt: 2026-10-17. Review: `relevant_passages_reviewed`; access: `retrieved`.

The archival biography records Obama’s inauguration on 20 January 2009 and return to private life on 20 January 2017.

Locator: Political Career and Post-Presidency.

Limitations: Chronology and initial pointers only. This institutional biography is not an independent assessment of leadership.

### BO02 — [Healthcare.gov: Ineffective Planning and Oversight Practices Underscore the Need for Improved Contract Management](https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-14-694)

US Government Accountability Office; GAO. Publication: 2014-07-30. Access attempt: 2026-10-17. Review: `official_summary_and_follow_up_reviewed`; access: `retrieved`.

GAO identified inadequate CMS planning and oversight, increased contract costs and delayed functionality. The record includes agency responses and later implementation of recommendations.

Locator: What GAO Found; Recommendations for Executive Action.

Limitations: The audit concerns CMS and contracts, not a finding of presidential personal misconduct. Separate the original audit from later updates and trace White House oversight and corrective decisions.

### BO03 — [Report on the Telephone Records Program Conducted under Section 215 and on the Operations of the Foreign Intelligence Surveillance Court](https://www.judiciary.senate.gov/imo/media/doc/PCLOBReport.pdf)

Privacy and Civil Liberties Oversight Board; PCLOB; copy hosted by US Senate Judiciary Committee. Publication: 2014-01-23. Access attempt: 2026-10-17. Review: `report_sections_reviewed`; access: `retrieved`.

The Board recommended ending bulk telephone-record collection, citing legal, privacy and effectiveness concerns. The report includes separate statements by two members.

Locator: Printed page 16, Recommendation 1; introduction and annex contents.

Limitations: This is an oversight board’s conclusion, not a criminal judgement. Separate statements require substantive reading; assess existing court authorisations, inherited arrangements and Obama’s response.

### BO04 — [Remarks by the President on Review of Signals Intelligence](https://obamawhitehouse.archives.gov/the-press-office/2014/01/17/remarks-president-review-signals-intelligence/)

Barack Obama; Office of the Press Secretary; Archived Obama White House. Publication: 2014-01-17. Access attempt: 2026-10-17. Review: `relevant_passages_reviewed`; access: `retrieved`.

Obama set out announced intelligence reforms and further privacy work, presenting the need to balance security and civil liberties.

Locator: Speech, reform proposals and closing discussion of privacy review.

Limitations: Primary account of announced policy, not independent proof of implementation. It precedes the PCLOB report; do not describe it as a response to that report.

### BO05 — [Remarks by the President at Sandy Hook Interfaith Prayer Vigil](https://obamawhitehouse.archives.gov/the-press-office/2012/12/16/remarks-president-sandy-hook-interfaith-prayer-vigil)

Barack Obama; Office of the Press Secretary; Archived Obama White House. Publication: 2012-12-16. Access attempt: 2026-10-17. Review: `relevant_passages_reviewed`; access: `retrieved`.

At the Newtown vigil Obama acknowledged bereaved families, praised responders and community support, and committed to work on preventing further shootings.

Locator: Speech at Newtown High School, passages on families and prevention.

Limitations: Evidence of public communication at one event. Seek family perspectives and follow-up actions; eloquence does not establish consistent empathy or successful prevention.

### BO06 — [Public Law 111–148 — Patient Protection and Affordable Care Act](https://www.govinfo.gov/app/details/PLAW-111publ148)

111th US Congress; US Government Publishing Office. Publication: 2010-03-23. Access attempt: 2026-10-17. Review: `publication_landing_page_reviewed`; access: `retrieved`.

The catalogue identifies the Patient Protection and Affordable Care Act as Public Law 111–148, dated 23 March 2010.

Locator: Content Details.

Limitations: The catalogue establishes the legislative record. The statute, implementation and distributional effects have not been fully reviewed. Passing legislation is not automatically evidence of service or sound process.

### BO07 — [Obama’s Numbers October 2016 Update](https://www.factcheck.org/2016/10/obamas-numbers-october-2016-update/)

Brooks Jackson; FactCheck.org, Annenberg Public Policy Center. Publication: 2016-10-09. Access attempt: 2026-10-17. Review: `relevant_passages_reviewed`; access: `retrieved`.

The analysis reports changes in health insurance coverage and other indicators, and warns that numerical changes do not determine presidential credit or blame.

Locator: Analysis introduction and Health Insurance section.

Limitations: Follow links to original Census and NHIS data. Definitions and measurement periods differ. Outcomes provide context rather than direct measures of the six leadership dimensions.
