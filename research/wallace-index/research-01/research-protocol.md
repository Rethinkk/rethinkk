# Research protocol — draft 01

## Scope and sampling

Research conduct in the specified office and assessment period. Earlier careers provide labelled context, not additional scored events. A source published later can establish facts about conduct within the period; distinguish the event date from publication, declaration and access dates. Do not treat registration after taking office as evidence that a benefit was received after taking office.

Before scoring, confirm exact period boundaries, constitutional powers and the methodology version. For Burnham, the working scope is the Greater Manchester mayoralty, not his ministerial career or any subsequent office. Different offices and term lengths require a visible contextual qualification; identical numerical scales do not establish equivalent opportunities or constraints.

For each leader, build a dated decision chronology covering the beginning, middle and end of the period. Search the same six dimensions for supporting, adverse and qualifying evidence. Include routine decisions as well as prominent controversies. Document search terms, repositories, exclusions and failed retrievals. Do not impose a quota of favourable or unfavourable items, or use source counts as score thresholds. This initial packet has not completed that systematic coverage.

## Evidence and claims

Keep the source's factual record separate from the researcher's interpretation and any later proposed rationale. Each claim must identify its source, a section or entry locator, the dimension it may inform and its limitations. A speech establishes the speaker's words and announced intentions. It does not establish implementation, sincerity or consistent conduct. A government announcement is not independent verification of its own success.

Institutional findings must be attributed to the issuing court, committee, inspectorate or auditor. Distinguish findings from allegations, proposals, government responses and dissenting opinions. Legal invalidity of a measure does not establish a criminal offence, dishonesty or a leader's private motives. Record the leader's contemporaneous response and later corrections. Where the original document is inaccessible, retain a discovery lead and identify any separately reviewed secondary account; do not imply the original was read.

Investigate individual responsibility: the decision taken, advice available, lawful authority, delegation, alternatives, subsequent oversight and response. An organisational failure or success is relevant context, not automatic personal blame or credit. Trace government-wide claims to the leader's documented conduct before using them to justify a score.

Use primary records alongside independent journalism, academic work and affected-community accounts. Follow statistical analysis back to original data and note changes in definitions. Syndicated versions of one wire report are one evidential origin. Official handout photographs distributed by a news agency do not become independent corroboration of wider claims. Preserve contradictory evidence and reasons for giving it different weight.

## Assessment and review

For each dimension, write an evidence synthesis and an explicit sufficiency judgement before proposing an integer score. Humility concerns observable responses to mistakes, scrutiny, credit and succession, not personality diagnosis or mannerisms. Service requires documented treatment of public duties and private interests; ideological agreement and good intentions do not establish it. Policy change may show learning or inconsistency: examine reasons and implementation. A forceful crisis statement alone does not prove either calm leadership or its absence.

No score is assigned while material evidence or attribution remains unresolved. Zero requires an assessed judgement under an approved rubric. All unassessed values remain null, and a total must remain null if any dimension is unassessed. Do not convert the legacy 0–3-like or larger values into new research judgements.

Next gates: complete source verification and period coverage; prepare proposed rationales; obtain explicit editorial approval of the operational rubric; assign an independent reviewer; record objections and required changes; obtain publisher approval; create an immutable edition through the established CMS. Research collection does not itself authorise publication. Do not claim endorsement by Ben Wallace.

## Preservation and remaining work

This packet preserves source metadata, passage locators, new summaries and explicit access limitations. It is not a byte-for-byte archive of the external publications. Before edition publication, capture permitted copies or stable archival references, record retrieval dates and checksums for retained files, and check that each cited passage can be recovered. Do not invent archive URLs or use a current dynamic page as an unlabelled historical snapshot.

Maintain a correction log and document new or conflicting evidence. Any later published correction must create a revision; it must not overwrite a previous edition. The lost provisional JSON and CSV remain unavailable. Their reconciliation cannot be described as complete and is not a prerequisite for this independent new research.
