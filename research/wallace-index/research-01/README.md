# The Wallace Index — renewed research, batch 01

Research date and evidence cut-off: 17 October 2026. Status: evidence collection, not an editorial assessment or published edition.

The commissioned subjects are Boris Johnson, Keir Starmer, Andy Burnham, Barack Obama and Olaf Scholz. The user confirmed Scholz, rather than Martin Schulz. Burnham's Greater Manchester mayoralty is the working office; other offices must be assessed separately. Precise period boundaries remain subject to editorial checking against dated institutional records.

This packet begins the replacement research after the loss of the provisional import files. It does not reconstruct missing original reasoning. The separate `../recovery-01/` archive preserves the historical prototype and its provenance. Its scores are not inputs to this research.

## Contents

- `research-register.json`: source records, bounded factual claims, five profiles and thirty dimension work items.
- `sources.csv`: portable source catalogue, including access failures and review limitations.
- `evidence-matrix.csv`: all thirty dimension work items and outstanding research.
- `dossiers/`: readable evidence summaries and questions for each leader.
- `research-protocol.md`: selection, attribution, comparison and editorial review rules.
- `validate.py`: structural integrity check; it does not verify political claims or approve assessments.

## What has and has not been established

Sources have been located and relevant passages or official summaries inspected where access succeeded. Each source records the extent of reading. Several sources are discovery leads only, with unsuccessful retrieval explicitly recorded. No full-report review is implied by reading an official summary. Source titles retain their original spelling and language; new editorial prose uses British English.

This is an initial evidence packet, not an exhaustive study of five complete terms. Independent corroboration, systematic coverage across each period, original decision records, affected-community evidence and responses to adverse findings remain necessary. The dossiers identify these gaps. All thirty scores and all five totals are null. Null means not assessed; it is not zero. There are no original or proposed numerical ratings in this packet.

The six dimensions and 0–3 scale remain unchanged. The operational rubric in `lib/wallace-index/methodology.ts` is version `1.0 — proposed` and is not approved. No assessor, independent reviewer or publisher has signed off these dossiers. The framework is qualitative, research-informed and not academically validated. The aggregate, when complete and approved, is descriptive rather than a scientific measurement.

Run `python3 research/wallace-index/research-01/validate.py` from the repository root. Nothing in this directory is connected to public routes, the CMS database or an automatic publishing workflow. No official assessment has been imported or published.
