## 2. Resolve the explicit 0/1 boundary

The descriptor “demonstrably negative” does not itself state how severe failures
and countervailing conduct should be balanced. Johnson Process 1 assumes that
formal adverse findings plus limited countervailing responses warrant the mixed
category. A reviewer may instead judge 0 justified by severity. The packet does
not claim that any apology automatically precludes 0, or that many ordinary acts
outweigh a serious violation. Record a reasoned case judgement; do not add an
unstated veto, point deduction or automatic disqualification.

For Johnson Humility, account for real acknowledgements without presuming their
sincerity or adequacy. An adverse Process finding may bear on acceptance of
scrutiny, but must not automatically determine another dimension.

## 3. Resolve the 1/2 boundary and coverage

Obama Humility 2 is a candidate based on personally attributable statements of
responsibility in different domains and credit to another office-holder. These
are substantive public acts, not proof of completed remedies or a full record
of conduct. Material early-term and contrary personal-accountability cases remain
to be tested. If reviewers regard the observations as isolated, withhold or amend
rather than promoting the number merely because several sources exist.

All four candidates retain the earlier whole-period sufficiency limitation.
This packet has not collected new evidence that removes it. It provides an
explicit numerical hypothesis for review, and should not be imported as an
accepted score. A reviewer must identify what evidence or coverage judgement
resolves each condition; agreement with a plausible number is not enough.

## 4. Allocate responsibility before attributing institutional failure

Scholz Process 1 assumes a head of government has relevant responsibility for a
government budget instrument, while preserving the distinction between that role
and personal authorship or knowing illegality. That assumption requires a stated
editorial decision and further review of the legal and ministerial record. The
ruling is not a personal dishonesty finding. Actual use of a constitutional
confidence procedure and the recorded handover are countervailing process
observations; losing a vote is not itself a negative index criterion.

Apply the same attribution discipline to Burnham and police operations, Obama
and agency delivery, and every government-wide outcome. No collective finding
is automatically a personal score.
