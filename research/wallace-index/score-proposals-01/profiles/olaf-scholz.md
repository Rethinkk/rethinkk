**Conditional proposal: 1 / 3 — Limited or inconsistent evidence**

The invalid supplementary-budget instrument is an established institutional failure, while Scholz’s direct response to the ruling, his recorded use of the Article 68 procedure and the documented handover show engagement with constitutional processes. A provisional 1 expresses the resulting mixed institutional record. Responsibility for governing within legal constraints is distinguished from an unproved claim that Scholz personally authored, knowingly violated or dishonestly evaded the budget rules.
