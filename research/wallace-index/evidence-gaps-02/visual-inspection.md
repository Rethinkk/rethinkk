# Visual inspection of scanned originals

The two Obama PDF scans produced no useful extracted text. They were therefore inspected as rendered pages; no automated OCR extraction or full asset review is claimed. Retained PDF hashes identify the originals, and the rendered PNG files are included with the evidence download.

## GS_BO01 — personal financial disclosure

Page 1 was inspected. It identifies Barack H. Obama as the incumbent filer and calendar year 2015 as the reporting year. The cover includes signatures. Other pages, asset details, valuations and the full certification process were not reviewed. The original posting date is not established from the filename. This supports only the cover-level claim in the register and cannot establish complete compliance or absence of interests.

## GS_BO04 — Nobel correspondence

All four pages were inspected:

1. Obama's signed letter dated 10 March 2010 directing transfer of the award to charities.
2. Designated recipients and transfer allocations.
3. Continuation of the recipient list and allocations.
4. Nobel Foundation confirmation dated 12 April 2010 stating that the proceeds were transferred on 18 March, with electronic transfers and a cheque distinguished.

The scoped claim relies principally on pages 1 and 4. The public source already contains redactions; none were removed or reconstructed. The Foundation letter is a documentary counterparty confirmation retained in the presidential archive, not a fresh response obtained from the Foundation or a claim that individual recipient bank accounts were inspected.
