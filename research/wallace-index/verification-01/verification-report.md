## Result and scope

The 28 original sources were successfully retrieved through direct HTTP requests with TLS verification. Six supplementary institutional records were also retrieved. The 24 bounded factual statements in research batch 01 are supported by their cited passages, with the qualifications preserved in the claim register. No existing numerical assessment has been verified, generated or published: the dossiers contain none.

The four former discovery leads are now readable originals. Six new bounded statements have been added from these sources. There are thirty checked statements in total. They state what the source records; they do not certify every wider claim within each publication, establish causation or constitute a judgement about a leader's character.

Every retrieved original has a UTC retrieval timestamp, final URL, content type, byte count and SHA-256 checksum. HTML text and PDF text were extracted for inspection. The source and text hashes make retained copies auditable. Passage locators identify the material actually checked. Full reports have not all been reviewed exhaustively, and reading an official summary is not described as reviewing the full underlying report.

## Attribution and contradictory evidence

For Johnson, the prorogation check uses the Supreme Court's own conclusion and remedy, rather than a party's allegation in the case history. The privileges finding remains attributed to the Committee. Later conduct during its inquiry cannot automatically enter a score for the premiership. The pandemic summary includes both strengths and weaknesses; collective performance is not automatically personal responsibility.

For Starmer, the parliamentary interests entry establishes what was declared and when, not a breach or improper influence. Receipt, acceptance and registration dates remain separate. The standards announcement proves the announced rules; enforcement has not been established. The Treasury benefits announcement cannot establish the Prime Minister's personal reasons for changing policy.

For Burnham, Kerslake sections 1.2–1.3 establish commissioning and the intended family focus. Section 2.22 contains positive accounts of personal visits. Section 2.23 records perceived differences in support for people outside Manchester. Neither set of experiences is treated as representative of every affected family, nor is a wider organisational failure assigned solely to the mayor. The original inspectorate letter records both improvement sufficient to end enhanced monitoring and work still to do. It is stronger corroboration of that decision than the force's announcement alone.

For Obama, the PCLOB conclusion is an oversight board's attributed assessment; it is not a criminal court judgement. Separate statements exist and still require substantive review. The intelligence speech on 17 January 2014 predates the 23 January report and must not be described as a response to it. GAO's original 2014 findings are distinguished from later recommendation-implementation updates. Population statistics do not themselves measure empathy or prove presidential causation.

For Scholz, the operative budget judgement confirms the invalidity finding described by the Court's press release. That is not a finding of personal dishonesty. The official security-policy speech establishes commitments, not delivery. A courteous handover is observable conduct but cannot by itself establish consistent humility or service.
