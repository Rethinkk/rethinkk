## Source checks

- **[AB01](https://www.greatermanchester-ca.gov.uk/news/greater-manchester-to-vote-for-new-mayor/)**: Previously inaccessible page now retrieved directly. It gives the vacancy date as 19 June 2026 and was published that day. The start date is separately supported by V03.
- **[AB02](https://www.greatermanchester-ca.gov.uk/media/1271/the-kerslake-report.pdf)**: PDF now retrieved. Sections 1.2–1.3 establish commissioning and intended family participation. Section 2.22 records positive visit experiences; section 2.23 records perceived geographical gaps in support. These accounts do not establish representativeness or sole mayoral responsibility.
- **[AB03](https://assets-hmicfrs.justiceinspectorates.gov.uk/uploads/an-inspection-of-the-service-provided-to-victims-of-crime-by-greater-manchester-police.pdf)**: The report confirms the September–October 2020 inspection and adverse as well as qualifying findings. Footer confirms December 2020, but an exact publication day is not established.
- **[AB04](https://www.gmp.police.uk/news/greater-manchester/news/news/2022/october/greater-manchester-police-most-improved-force-in-the-country-as-chief-constable-and-mayor-confirm-hmicfrs-has-removed-force-from-special-measures/)**: The institutional account is now corroborated on the narrow removal-from-monitoring decision by the inspector’s original letter V05. Other performance figures and personal credit remain separately unverified.
- **[AB05](https://www.greatermanchester-ca.gov.uk/what-we-do/transport/bus-franchising/)**: Official overview now retrieved. It records the March 2021 decision and rollout dates. Its budget, patronage and comparative claims are not independently validated here.

## Bounded statements supported

- **AB03-C1**: The inspection identified serious weaknesses in victim service, crime recording, investigation and governance, while recognising some improvements and a programme of change. Locator: Printed pages 8–12: summary, supervision and governance, causes of concern; inspection conducted September–October 2020.
- **AB04-C1**: The police force announced removal from enhanced monitoring and described improvements in call handling, recording and investigation. Locator: Opening announcement and crime-recording/investigation passages.
- **AB01-C1**: The GMCA notice states that the mayoral vacancy occurred on 19 June 2026 after Burnham’s election as an MP. Locator: Vacancy notice, published 19 June 2026.
- **AB02-C1**: The independent review records that Burnham commissioned it and prioritised the experiences of bereaved families and other affected people. Locator: Sections 1.2–1.3, printed pages 11–12.
- **AB02-C2**: The report records some respondents’ positive accounts of personal visits by the mayor and chief constable. Locator: Section 2.22, printed pages 49–50.
- **AB02-C3**: The report also records that some respondents outside Manchester felt overlooked or less well supported. This is not a finding of Burnham’s sole responsibility. Locator: Section 2.23, printed page 50.
- **AB05-C1**: GMCA records Burnham’s decision to introduce bus franchising in March 2021, with the first services running in September 2023 and final rollout in January 2025. Locator: The journey to franchised bus services.
