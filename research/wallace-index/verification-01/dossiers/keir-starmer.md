## Source checks

- **[KS01](https://www.gov.uk/government/people/keir-starmer)**: Both boundaries agree with UK Parliament career record V02. This is cross-checking two institutional records, not certification of every biography statement.
- **[KS02](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/new-ministerial-code-to-set-out-standards-in-public-life-and-restore-transparency)**: The dated announcement supports monthly disclosure and the no-veto investigation provision. Enforcement and actual compliance remain unverified.
- **[KS03](https://publications.parliament.uk/pa/cm/cmregmem/241014/starmer_keir.htm)**: The Arsenal entry confirms receipt/acceptance on 17 August and registration on 10 September. Other benefits predate office or straddle it; no breach finding is inferred.
- **[KS04](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/plan-for-change)**: Publication date, CP number and presentation to Parliament are confirmed on the landing page. The attachment is not represented as substantively reviewed.
- **[KS05](https://www.gov.uk/government/speeches/pm-statement-4-august-2024)**: The transcript supports the limited description of words delivered. Operational outcomes and effects on communities remain outside this source’s proof.
- **[KS06](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/nine-million-pensioners-to-receive-winter-fuel-payments-this-winter)**: Treasury announcement confirms the threshold and the narrower 2024/25 background. Attribution to Starmer’s personal reasoning remains unsupported.

## Bounded statements supported

- **KS01-C1**: The retrieved biography states that Starmer was Prime Minister from 5 July 2024 to 20 July 2026. Locator: Biography.
- **KS02-C1**: The announcement sets out monthly publication of ministerial gifts and hospitality, including values, and an independent adviser’s power to initiate investigations without a prime ministerial veto. Locator: Gifts and hospitality rules; independent adviser powers.
- **KS03-C1**: The register includes Arsenal tickets and hospitality received on 17 August 2024 and declared on 10 September 2024. Other entries concern earlier dates or periods straddling the start of the premiership. Locator: Category 3; Arsenal entry received 17 August 2024; accommodation entry 29 May–13 July 2024.
- **KS04-C1**: The publication record identifies the Plan for Change and states it was presented to Parliament on 5 December 2024. Locator: Details and document metadata: CP 1210, ISBN 978-1-5286-5291-9.
- **KS05-C1**: Starmer condemned violent disorder, referred to targeted Muslim and other minority communities, and stated that the government would support the police and pursue criminal enforcement. Locator: Speech transcript, paragraphs on targeted communities and policing.
- **KS06-C1**: The Treasury announced expanded Winter Fuel Payment eligibility in England and Wales using a £35,000 income threshold, following the narrower arrangements introduced for winter 2024/25. Locator: Eligibility announcement and Further background.
