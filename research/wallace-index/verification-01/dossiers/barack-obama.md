# Barack Obama — verification addendum

Checked 17 October 2026 against research batch 01, commit 1430904. This addendum supersedes that batch’s access and period uncertainties where explicitly resolved below. Scores remain unassessed; no total or editorial approval.

Working assessment office: President. Boundaries supported by checked records: 2009-01-20 to 2017-01-20. Sources: BO01. Different offices and term lengths still require contextual comparison.

## Source checks

- **[BO01](https://obamalibrary.archives.gov/obamas/president-barack-obama)**: Only the inauguration and return-to-private-life dates were used. No other biographical claim is admitted through this verification.
- **[BO02](https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-14-694)**: GAO highlights confirm planning and oversight weaknesses, cost increases and delayed functionality. Dynamic follow-up records implemented recommendations; that is separate from the 2014 original finding.
- **[BO03](https://www.judiciary.senate.gov/imo/media/doc/PCLOBReport.pdf)**: Recommendation 1 supports the attributed legal/privacy/value concerns; contents identify separate member statements. They must be read before treating the conclusions as unanimous. This is not a judicial criminal finding.
- **[BO04](https://obamawhitehouse.archives.gov/the-press-office/2014/01/17/remarks-president-review-signals-intelligence/)**: Reform announcements and privacy work are in the 17 January speech. The speech predates the 23 January PCLOB report and is not described as a response to it.
- **[BO05](https://obamawhitehouse.archives.gov/the-press-office/2012/12/16/remarks-president-sandy-hook-interfaith-prayer-vigil)**: Transcript confirms the limited account of acknowledgement, praise and commitments. Empathy over the whole term and prevention outcomes are not established.
- **[BO06](https://www.govinfo.gov/app/details/PLAW-111publ148)**: GovInfo catalogue confirms law identifier and 23 March 2010 date. No substantive statutory or implementation conclusion is inferred from metadata.
- **[BO07](https://www.factcheck.org/2016/10/obamas-numbers-october-2016-update/)**: The analysis discusses insurance indicators and explicitly cautions against automatic presidential credit/blame. Original datasets and causal attribution remain outstanding.

## Bounded statements supported

- **BO01-C1**: The archival biography records Obama’s inauguration on 20 January 2009 and return to private life on 20 January 2017. Locator: Political Career and Post-Presidency.
- **BO02-C1**: GAO identified inadequate CMS planning and oversight, increased contract costs and delayed functionality. The record includes agency responses and later implementation of recommendations. Locator: What GAO Found; Recommendations for Executive Action.
- **BO03-C1**: The Board recommended ending bulk telephone-record collection, citing legal, privacy and effectiveness concerns. The report includes separate statements by two members. Locator: Printed page 16, Recommendation 1; introduction and annex contents.
- **BO04-C1**: Obama set out announced intelligence reforms and further privacy work, presenting the need to balance security and civil liberties. Locator: Speech, reform proposals and closing discussion of privacy review.
- **BO05-C1**: At the Newtown vigil Obama acknowledged bereaved families, praised responders and community support, and committed to work on preventing further shootings. Locator: Speech at Newtown High School, passages on families and prevention.
- **BO06-C1**: The catalogue identifies the Patient Protection and Affordable Care Act as Public Law 111–148, dated 23 March 2010. Locator: Content Details.
- **BO07-C1**: The analysis reports changes in health insurance coverage and other indicators, and warns that numerical changes do not determine presidential credit or blame. Locator: Analysis introduction and Health Insurance section.
